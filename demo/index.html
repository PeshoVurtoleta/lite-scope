The protocol home of the profiler suite: a registry, sinks, and a timebase — no producers here. Two demo streams write records through a channel.write(op, t, a, b); strings are interned to u32 ids at init and ride slot b, never the wire. The burst button overruns the ring so you can watch overwrite-oldest and the overflow counter climb. Every record is decoded back through readSlab, the same reference decoder probe repos vendor.
